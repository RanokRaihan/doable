Common questions

Two sides, same answers.

Whatever side of a task you're on, here's what folks ask the most before they sign up.

I'm posting a task

Questions from people getting help.

Posting is free. You only pay the agreed amount when the task is complete, plus a small 5% service fee that keeps the lights on.
Every helper is ID-verified, and you can see their ratings, reviews, and completed task history before you pick anyone.
98% of tasks get at least one applicant within 2 hours. If yours doesn't, we'll suggest tweaks to your budget or timing — or refund any boost you bought.
You can cancel free up until they arrive. After that, a small fee covers their travel time. Full details in our cancellation policy.
Funds sit in escrow and only release when you confirm the task is done. If there's an issue, our team mediates within 24 hours.

I'm applying for tasks

Questions from people helping out.

Funds release the moment the poster confirms — typically within 5 minutes of finishing — and land in your bank within 1-2 business days.
If a poster doesn't confirm 24 hours after you mark a task complete, payment auto-releases. You're never on the hook for their delays.
